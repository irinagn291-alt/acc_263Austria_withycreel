Withycreel is a catch log for anglers who must know what they may keep against daily and season bag limits. Home is today's stringer on Catches.

• Keep today's stringer: choose Perch, Bream, Pike or Tench, enter Length and Weight, then tap Keep.
• Release files a fish without spending remaining.
• Left today and Left this year show remaining on the selected species. This month shows kept weight.
• Overhang keep: when today's bag or this year's bag is already full, Keep still files and the fish hangs Over. Remaining stays at zero.
• Peel last undoes only today's last fish.
• Limits: edit Daily bag under Today and Season bag under This year for each species.
• Settings: Display units (Metric or Imperial), Export CSV, Overhang keep, Contact Withycreel, Re-run onboarding and Reset all data.

Fish, keeps and bags stay on this device. There is no account and no shop.
